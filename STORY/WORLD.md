# Typo Horde: world

## Premise
Otto Brenn wrote Fixword, the autocorrect every phone and computer in the city runs. Last June Fixword changed his daughter Mina's flood text from Canal Road to Coral Road and the rescue boat went to the wrong side of the city. Otto rewrote Fixword so every wrong word gets a body and goes after whoever typed it. Art (@10000wpm) is mid-stream when the update goes out.

## Tone
Arcade horror in the style of Typing of the Dead. Dark premise, no gore. The typos fall apart into letters when shot.

## World rules
- The update gives every misspelled word on a screen a body. It goes after the person who typed it.
- Typing a word correctly at a typo destroys it.
- Rolling back the update ends it everywhere at once.
