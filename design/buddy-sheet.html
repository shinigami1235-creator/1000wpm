> Tilde

The buddy for 10000WPM is a small floating keycap with a screen for a face and a text caret for an antenna. It lives beside the page, follows the part you are reading and reacts to your typing.

Every expression and pose below is drawn from the same parts and they blend into each other on the site instead of cutting.
